Most drain calls we run in Prescott fall into two categories: a single fixture clog from grease, hair, or soap buildup, and a main line clog further downstream, often tied to root intrusion in an older clay sewer lateral. We identify which one you actually have before quoting a fix, since the tools and the price for each are very different.

Why Drains Clog More In Older Prescott Neighborhoods

Homes built before the 1970s near downtown and Miller Valley often still run on original clay or cast iron sewer laterals. Both materials are prone to root intrusion from mature trees, and a partial root blockage tends to clog repeatedly at the same spot every few months rather than clearing for good with a simple snake.

Newer construction toward Prescott Valley and the east side of town runs PVC lateral lines, which resist root intrusion far better but can still clog from grease, wipes, or construction debris left in the line during a build. Knowing which material you have changes whether a cable clears it permanently or just buys a few months.

Signs You Need This Service.

  • Water backing up in a tub or shower when the washing machine drains.
  • A gurgling sound from a drain when another fixture in the house is used.
  • Slow drainage in more than one fixture at the same time, a sign of a main line issue.
  • Sewage odor near a floor drain or cleanout in the yard.
  • A drain that clogs again within weeks of a prior cleaning.

How We Find The Actual Clog

A single slow fixture usually gets a hand-fed cable first. When more than one fixture is affected, or a fixture has clogged repeatedly, we run a sewer camera down the line to see the actual blockage, whether that is root intrusion, a bellied section of pipe, or built-up grease, before recommending a fix. Guessing at a repair without seeing the line first is how a homeowner ends up paying for the same clog twice.

Cabling vs. Hydro Jetting

A standard cable clears most single-fixture clogs and light root intrusion. A line with heavy grease buildup or repeated root regrowth needs more than a cable. Hydro jetting scours the full diameter of the pipe with high-pressure water rather than just punching a hole through the blockage, which is why it tends to hold longer on a line that keeps clogging.

One-Time Cable vs. Hydro Jetting

Cable Only

  • Lower cost per visit.
  • Punches a path through the clog rather than clearing the full pipe wall.
  • Root regrowth often returns within months.
  • Fine for a single, isolated fixture clog.

Hydro Jetting

  • Clears grease and roots from the full pipe diameter.
  • Holds longer on lines with a history of repeat clogs.
  • Works from an existing yard cleanout in most cases.
  • Recommended once camera inspection shows heavy buildup.

Before We Arrive For A Backed-Up Drain.

  • Stop running water into any fixture connected to the affected drain, including the washing machine.
  • Do not pour chemical drain cleaner into a fixture that is already backing up. It can splash back and it will not clear a main line clog.
  • If sewage is backing up into a floor drain or tub, keep the area clear of foot traffic until it is resolved.
  • Note which fixtures are affected. Whether it is one sink or several tells us a lot before we even arrive.
