Where Prescott Lakes Is

Prescott Lakes is a large master-planned community on the northeast side of Prescott. A local real estate guide places it between Willow Creek Reservoir and Watson Lake, with Willow Creek Road on its west edge, State Route 89 on the east, and Prescott Lakes Parkway among its main roads. Sources describe it as more than 1,000 acres, with custom homes, patio homes, townhomes, and condos in a mix of gated and non-gated sections.

A Community Built In Phases

The Prescott Lakes Community Association was formed in 1996, according to its listing in the Arizona HOA registry, and the community has been built out in phases since. The Club at Prescott Lakes, an 18-hole golf course, is part of the community. Real estate sources disagree about typical build years, so we do not assume the age of any house here. We ask, check the water heater label, and go from there.

Gated Sections And Access

Some sections of Prescott Lakes are gated and others are not. If you live behind a gate, tell us when you call which gate to use, any code or guard procedure, and the exact address, so a visit does not stall at the entrance. If you manage a rental, have the access details ready for the tenant before we are scheduled.

Community Association Rules

Associations often have their own rules for contractors, work hours, and exterior changes. We do not know the specific rules of the Prescott Lakes association, so check with it before scheduling work that changes the outside of a home, such as a new vent, an exposed pipe, or equipment placement.

Plumbing Services For Homes Like These

A water heater that is failing or leaking is the call many homeowners make first, and our water heater repair and water heater replacement pages explain both paths. The City of Prescott's 2026 permit list says water heater replacement needs a permit, so we plan for that. If you are weighing a tankless unit, see tankless water heater service.

For a water bill that rises with no obvious cause, start with the meter test. Shut off every fixture and watch whether the meter's leak indicator keeps moving. If it does, our leak detection service finds the source without guesswork. Slow drains and backups are covered on our drain cleaning page.

Before We Arrive: A Leak Or No Hot Water

  • For an active leak, shut off the main water valve if you can reach it safely.
  • If the water heater is leaking, turn off its power or gas control and shut off its cold water supply.
  • Have your gate code or guard-house instructions ready and tell us which entrance to use.
  • Call us and give the address, the symptoms, and the age of the water heater if you know it.
