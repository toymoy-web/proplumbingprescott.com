Where Yavapai Hills Is

Yavapai Hills sits on the east side of Prescott off State Route 69, with Yavapai Hills Drive, Sunrise Boulevard, and Sharp Shooter Way among its streets. A local real estate guide puts downtown about five miles away and describes the neighborhood as first developed in the 1970s and added to in later decades, with roughly 740 homes in a mix of single-family houses, townhouses, and a few condos. Those are real estate figures rather than official counts, so treat them as a rough picture.

The City's Yavapai Hills Lift Station Project

The City of Prescott owns a Yavapai Hills Lift Station at 5101 Cactus Place. According to the City's construction drawings (capital project 2105-004, dated February 2024, funded through Arizona's Water Infrastructure Finance Authority), the existing station is to be replaced. The plans include new wet wells and pumps, a standby generator, and temporary bypass lines to keep sewage flowing during the work.

The drawings do not say which streets the station serves, and we do not know the project schedule, so contact the City's utility for current status. It is a useful reminder that a sewer system has public parts and private parts, and a backup can start in either.

Is It Your Drain Or The City's Sewer?

As general guidance, one slow fixture usually points to a clog in that fixture's own drain. Several drains backing up at once, or a toilet that gurgles when the washing machine empties, points toward the main line. If neighbors report the same thing, call the City's utility as well, because the blockage may be in a public main. We run a camera from your cleanout and show you what is there, which settles whether the problem is on your side. Ask the City where its responsibility ends and yours begins, since that boundary is the City's to explain. Our drain cleaning and sewer line repair pages cover what we do once the cause is clear.

A Neighborhood Built In Stages

Because Yavapai Hills was added to over several decades, houses of different ages sit side by side. A 1980s home and a 2010s home on the same street can have different water heaters, fixtures, and piping, so we go by the house in front of us. Check the age of your house and the date on the water heater's label, and we will take it from there with water heater repair or water heater replacement.

Remodels follow the same approach. If a new kitchen layout changes how a sink drains, our garbage disposal service and faucet and sink repair work starts with how the existing lines are routed.

Before We Arrive: A Backup Or Slow Main Drain

  • Stop running water, including the washing machine and dishwasher, until the line is cleared.
  • Note which drains are affected and whether a toilet bubbles when another fixture drains.
  • Ask a neighbor whether they have the same problem. If so, tell the City's utility as well as us.
  • Call us and give the address and the symptoms so we can plan the right equipment.
