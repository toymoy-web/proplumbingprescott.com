This is a heavier-duty service than a routine drain cleaning call, aimed at lines with an established buildup problem rather than a first-time isolated clog. We use it for both residential lines with recurring issues and commercial lines with regular grease or scale accumulation.

Where Jetting Makes The Real Difference

A standard cable clears a path through a blockage but leaves material coating the pipe walls behind, which is exactly what allows a grease-heavy kitchen line or a root-affected clay lateral to clog again within weeks or months. Jetting's high-pressure water scours the full circumference of the pipe, removing that residual buildup rather than just punching through the middle of it.

Prescott's historic core has real root intrusion concerns in older clay and cast iron sewer laterals. Commercial kitchens along the Mingus Avenue corridor and downtown restaurant row generate the kind of ongoing grease buildup that a periodic jetting schedule handles far better than reactive cabling after each clog.

Signs You Need This Service.

  • A drain that clogs again within weeks of a prior cleaning.
  • A commercial kitchen line with a history of grease-related slowdowns.
  • Root intrusion confirmed by camera inspection in an older sewer lateral.
  • Multiple fixtures draining slowly at once, suggesting a main line issue.
  • A line that a standard cable repeatedly fails to fully clear.

Confirming Jetting Is The Right Call.

We run a camera inspection before recommending jetting whenever the line's condition is not already well established, since an already-fragile or severely deteriorated clay line can sometimes be too weak for aggressive water pressure without confirming its structural condition first. For a known grease or root problem, this step is sometimes skipped in favor of moving straight to the jetting appointment.

Residential Versus Commercial Jetting

A residential jetting visit typically addresses one problem line, often the kitchen or main sewer line, as a one-time or occasional service. Commercial jetting is often set up as a recurring maintenance schedule, quarterly or semi-annually depending on the business's grease output, to prevent the kind of buildup that leads to an emergency closure if a line backs up during business hours.

One-Time Jetting Vs. A Maintenance Schedule

One-Time Service

  • Addresses a current, established buildup problem.
  • Lower total cost than a recurring contract.
  • Buildup can recur over time without follow-up.
  • Reasonable for a residential line with an isolated history.

Recurring Commercial Schedule

  • Prevents buildup from reaching problem levels.
  • Avoids the cost and disruption of an emergency backup.
  • Higher total cost over a year than a single visit.
  • Standard for commercial kitchens with regular grease output.

What Not To Do: Why This Is Not A DIY Service.

  • Hydro jetting equipment operates at pressures high enough to cause serious injury if mishandled, which is why it is always operated by a trained technician, never a homeowner.
  • Keep the work area, including any open cleanout, clear of children and pets while jetting is underway.
  • A cracked or already-fragile pipe can fail under jetting pressure, which is why we camera-inspect an unknown line's condition first.
  • Never attempt to rent jetting equipment for DIY use on a residential line without professional guidance.
